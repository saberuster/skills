# Patterns and counterexamples

Use the relevant examples to recognize a problem, not to run replacements over
every draft. These examples are synthetic. The skill's preservation rules take
priority over a stylistic improvement.

## Empty evaluation

Before: "Our remarkable export feature empowers users to download CSV files."

Possible edit: "Users can download CSV files."

Keep an evaluation when it expresses the author's actual judgment or requested
voice. Never replace missing evidence with an invented benefit, metric, or
feature. "Experts say exports are 50% faster" needs support; deleting "Experts
say" would make the unsupported claim stronger.

## Redundancy and framing

Before: "In order to retry, click Retry. Clicking Retry retries the request."

Possible edit: "To retry the request, click Retry."

Keep repetition that helps a reader learn, warns at the point of danger, or is
deliberate emphasis. A courteous sign-off in an email can belong to the writer;
it is not automatically chatbot residue.

## Forced structure

Before: "The archive is not just a file, but a file containing the logs."

Possible edit: "The archive contains the logs."

Keep a real distinction: "The archive contains logs, not uploaded files."
Likewise, a list of exactly three supported formats needs no repair. Repeating
the same technical term can be clearer than cycling through synonyms.

## Overworked or unclear sentences

Before: "The loader performs the parsing of the file before it initiates the
validation of its records."

Possible edit: "The loader parses the file, then validates its records."

Only name an actor or sequence supported by the original. "The incident was
resolved at 09:10" does not say who resolved it. "Latency fell after the rollout"
does not establish that the rollout caused the reduction. "The cache may reduce
latency" must not become "The cache reduces latency."

## Formatting and language habits

Repeated bold labels, decorative symbols, or uniform sentence patterns may
distract, but preserve formatting that helps readers navigate or matches a
requested style. Punctuation can express a relationship or preserve rhythm;
there is no universal ban on dashes, parentheses, colons, or passive voice.

For Chinese prose, look for translation-like syntax, stacked abstract phrases,
empty slogans, and repeated connective scaffolding. Use natural Chinese
phrasing while preserving stance and register. Do not translate an English
word blacklist, enforce English punctuation, or change established terminology.
For mixed-language text, retain intentional language choices and exact terms.
Treat locale-specific typography as a project convention, not an AI detector.

## When leaving the text alone is better

"Requests may be retried only after the server returns 503."

This is already precise. Removing "may", "only", or the condition would change
the contract. Do not shorten it merely to show that editing happened.

"I liked the demo. I still do not trust it with my notes."

The tension is the author's position. Do not supply a reason, replace it with
balanced marketing language, or manufacture a personal experience to add voice.
